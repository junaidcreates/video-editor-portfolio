About me

From graphic design to storytelling through video.

I'm Junaid, a 20-year-old Video Editor & Visual Storyteller with 4+ years of experience in editing and visual content.

My passion for video editing and graphic design started when I was in 8th class. I began by making videos for my mother's YouTube channel, experimenting with visuals, cuts and simple edits. What started as something I enjoyed doing gradually became a serious creative interest.

As I kept creating, I became more interested in how editing could shape a story. I started exploring pacing, transitions, sound design, motion graphics and different visual treatments. Over time, I learned that good editing isn't about adding the most effects; it's about knowing what the story needs, what the viewer needs to see, and when.

My professional experience with Arkintel, a U.S.-based company, gave me the opportunity to apply that approach to long-form educational and information-driven content. Across 200+ videos, I've worked with different subjects and editing styles while keeping the focus on clarity and viewer engagement.
